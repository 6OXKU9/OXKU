S&P 500 rebounds, eyeing 7,716

bullishTimeframe D1Published 10 Nov 2026Result: open

Summary

The S&P 500 gained 2.3% on Monday, the most since April, and closed at 7,550. Unless the index closes a day below Monday's low of 7,422, a rise to the 4 November high near 7,716 is the base case.
